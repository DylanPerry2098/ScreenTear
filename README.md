## Illuminate the Unknown.
**FlashPoint** is a game where not everything is as it seems.  Use your *flashlight* to navigate a pitch black environment and find the exit. 
But be wary, you don't know what's shifting in the darkness. From creepy crawlies to the environment itself, you can only trust what's in the light. 
Stay vigilant, watch your back, and illuminate the path ahead.

In **FlashPoint**, you must use your flashlight to solve puzzles in order to progress. There are many things that become collidable, disappear, or freeze when in the light.
Some levels feature pressure plates that must be activated to enable overhead lights or open doors, or blocks that you can't pass through until you've looked at them.
